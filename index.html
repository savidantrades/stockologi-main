The rules

Rational. Transparent. Irresponsible on purpose.

We print the downside

Every name ships with the risks the bulls skip: dilution, debt, cash burn, thin volume. If it costs us a reader, fine.

We keep score in public

The index tracks every pick against the benchmark. Bad calls stay up. That is the whole point.

Paid means labelled

If a company ever pays for placement, the post says so, up top, with what was paid. Editorial picks are never for sale.
